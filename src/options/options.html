DOMLens

Hold the configured hotkey and move your mouse over elements. The hovered element is highlighted and an info panel shows the tag, classes, dimensions, position, colors, and typography. Releasing the hotkey hides everything. Press C while holding the hotkey to copy a unique CSS selector (plus any visible text) to the clipboard — the highlight briefly flashes green to confirm. Press C twice in quick succession to copy a full snapshot of the element (HTML, box, computed styles, and text).

Hotkey
